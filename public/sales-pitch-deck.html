*100% silk*


Since this is your first style with us, here's how the whole process runs, start to finish.


*STEP 1 — SAMPLING — ₹12,000 (one-time)*

- Pattern making + grading across your size range

*PRINT ARTWORK DEVELOPMENT — ₹15,000 to ₹40,000 (one-time, separate)*

This is an engineered print, not a repeat — every panel carries its own designed zone, with borders landing deliberately at the tier hems and sleeve edges. That artwork has to be built and laid out per pattern piece, per size. It's a one-time investment and the file remains yours for all future styles in this print.


Not applicable if you'd rather we source an existing engineered print.


*STEP 2 — BULK — ₹1,340 per piece (25 pieces)*

Plus the one-time ₹12,000 sampling and the artwork development above.


*WHY THIS STYLE COSTS WHAT IT DOES*

Almost all of it is the silk and the print.


Placement cutting is wasteful by nature. Each tier has to be cut where its border sits on the cloth, so panels can't be nested freely — that pushes consumption to six and a half metres, roughly a metre more than the same dress would need in an ordinary repeat print. On silk at this quality, that extra metre is the single biggest line in the quote.


On the making side, the beaded trim runs along the V neckline, over both shoulders and around both sleeve edges — close to two metres per dress, hand-anchored rather than glued. The sleeve ruffles then carry fine rolled hems, which on silk is slow, careful work.


*WORTH KNOWING*

- *Silk cannot be unpicked.* Needle holes and pin marks are permanent, so nothing on this garment gets a second attempt. This is why the sample is signed off fully before bulk begins, and why the strike-off comes first

- *The strike-off matters more here than on any other fabric.* Fine gold scrollwork and layered florals shift noticeably between screen and silk — silk takes dye differently to every other cloth, and the colours come up richer and deeper. You approve the print on the actual silk, not on a proof

- Engineered printing carries a minimum, usually 50 to 100 metres per design. At 25 pieces you need around 165 metres, so you clear it comfortably

- Silk frays quickly, so all seams are French-seamed or clean-bound; nothing is overlocked raw

- Tiers are hung overnight before their hems are finished. On a full silk maxi the bias settles considerably, and an unhung hem sits visibly uneven once the dress relaxes

- Beads are anchored individually, never glued. On silk a glued bead line also marks the fabric permanently

- Pressing is steam only, at low heat, through a cloth — silk glazes and the mark doesn't come out


*TWO THINGS TO CONFIRM*

- *Artwork route* — original engineered artwork developed for you, or an existing engineered print sourced off the shelf. This is the widest variable in the whole quote

- *Quantity and size split* — quoted at 25, let us know your actual numbers. At 50 pieces the making charge comes down to around ₹1,150 and the silk rate improves meaningfully


*TIMELINE*

- Print artwork development — 15-20 days

- Strike-off on silk — 7-8 days

- Sample after strike-off approval — 14-16 days

- Correction round — 6-7 days

- Bulk printing — 10-12 days

- Bulk production, 25 pcs — 25-30 days from fabric in hand


*HOW WE WORK*

- Once confirmed, you're added to a dedicated group with our designers and production team

- Every update, image and decision happens there — you'll see the artwork and the style progress at each stage

- Silk, printing and trims are purchased only after your written approval on the options shared, with photos and videos of each


*TERMS*

- Sampling and artwork — 100% advance

- Bulk — 50% advance, 50% before dispatch

- Quotation valid 15 days
